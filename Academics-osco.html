Academic Competitions

Because we believe education is the key to success, we participate in key educational competitions that build the mind of our students and make them sharper than they usually would be.

National Science & Math Quiz

Oda Senior High School (ODASCO) has made significant strides in the National Science and Maths Quiz (NSMQ), demonstrating the school’s commitment to academic excellence and competitive growth. A historic moment came in 2021 when ODASCO advanced to the quarterfinals for the first time after defeating Nifa SHS and Boso SHTS in the one-eighth stage. Although their journey ended in the quarterfinals against Opoku Ware School and Ghana SHS, Koforidua, their performance was a source of pride and inspiration. In 2023, ODASCO participated in the preliminary stage, competing strongly but narrowly missing qualification for the next round. Through determination and resilience, ODASCO continues to build a legacy of excellence in the NSMQ, motivating students to strive for greater achievements.

  • 2021 – Progressed to the Quarter finals stage.
  • 2023 – Progressed to the national preliminary stage.

The National Cyber Security Challenge

Oda Senior High School (ODASCO) has been an active participant in the National Cybersecurity Challenge, a flagship initiative by Ghana’s Cyber Security Authority to promote cybersecurity awareness among students. In the inaugural 2022 competition, ODASCO competed against prestigious schools across the country, earning a commendable fifth place with 50 points. Building on this foundation, the school participated in the 2023 Eastern Zone competition, showcasing enthusiasm and a strong commitment to enhancing digital safety skills. ODASCO’s involvement in the challenge underscores its dedication to equipping students with essential cybersecurity knowledge and fostering a culture of responsible online behavior, contributing to a safer digital future for Ghana.

  • 2022 – Finals.
  • 2023 – Zonals.
  • 2024 – Regionals.

The First lego League

Oda Senior High School (ODASCO) has been actively involved in the FIRST LEGO League (FLL), a global robotics competition that encourages students to engage with STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) through hands-on learning. In 2022, ODASCO participated in the competition held at Galaxy International School, showcasing their talent and innovative thinking in the realm of robotics. The team competed alongside other schools, tackling real-world challenges with creativity and teamwork. Building on this experience, ODASCO continued to make strides in 2024, participating in the FLL competition at the University of Ghana (UG) Legon. These experiences demonstrate ODASCO’s commitment to fostering critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and leadership qualities among students, preparing them for the future in an increasingly technological world.

  • 2022
  • 2024

About Us

Oda Senior High School (ODASCO), located in Akyem Oda in Ghana's Eastern Region, is a respected institution known for academic excellence and discipline. Since its establishment in 1960, ODASCO has provided a supportive environment for learning and personal growth, empowering students to excel and contribute to society with integrity and dedication.
