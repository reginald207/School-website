About Us

Oda Senior High School (ODASCO), located in Akyem Oda in Ghana's Eastern Region, is a respected institution known for academic excellence and discipline. Since its establishment in 1960, ODASCO has provided a supportive environment for learning and personal growth, empowering students to excel and contribute to society with integrity and dedication.
