    ODA SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL CROWNED REGIONAL DEBATE CHAMPIONS FOR THE SECOND TIME

    Oda Senior High School (Odasco) has once again demonstrated its intellectual excellence by securing the top spot in the regional debate competition. This remarkable achievement reinforces the school’s reputation as a leading academic institution in the region.

    The competition featured some of the brightest minds from various schools, each team delivering compelling arguments and showcasing outstanding public speaking skills. Despite the fierce contest, Odasco emerged victorious with an impressive 32 points, claiming the coveted championship title for the second time.

    Final Standings:

    • Ghanass: 26 points
    • Presec Mampong: 28 points
    • Kade SHS: 29 points
    • Akim Asafo: 30 points
    • Odasco: 32 points

    This victory is a moment of pride for the entire Odasco community, highlighting the dedication, hard work, and perseverance of the students, teachers, and staff. Their commitment to academic excellence continues to yield remarkable results.

    As regional champions, Odasco will now advance to the national debate competition, where they will compete against the best schools from across the country.

    We extend our heartfelt congratulations to the team and wish them success as they represent the region at the national level!

      About Us

      Oda Senior High School (ODASCO), located in Akyem Oda in Ghana's Eastern Region, is a respected institution known for academic excellence and discipline. Since its establishment in 1960, ODASCO has provided a supportive environment for learning and personal growth, empowering students to excel and contribute to society with integrity and dedication.
